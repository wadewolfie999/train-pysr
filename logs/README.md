Logs capture commands, environments, warnings, failures, and transcripts.

All logs and transcripts present before the REBUILD baseline, including
A4-Lite materials, are preserved historical records. Their original bytes and
provenance are non-controlling and must not be treated as an appointment,
execution contract, evidence acceptance, verdict, closure, transition, or LOCK.

The directional correction for `VERBATIM-A1-TO-E1-CONTEXT` remains rebuild
backlog unless A1 explicitly adopts it. Do not rewrite the original transcript
bytes.
